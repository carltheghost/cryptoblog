WIZARD'S CHESS: SPELLBOUND
==========================
Real chess PLUS battle magic. New idea: you earn MANA each turn and spend it on
spells. The stone army (Black) casts spells too!

TO PLAY:
 - Easiest: double-click  Spellbound.html  (opens in your browser, no install)
 - Localhost: double-click "Play on localhost (Windows).bat", then open
   http://localhost:8801

SPELLS (cost mana ◆; casting uses your turn):
  Fire Bolt  (5) — destroy any enemy piece except the King
  Freeze     (3) — an enemy piece can't move for 2 turns
  Shield     (2) — your piece can't be captured for 2 turns
  Apparate   (4) — teleport your piece to any empty square (click piece, then empty square)
  Summon     (4) — conjure a pawn on an empty square in your half

HOW: click a spell in the Spellbook, then click a glowing target. Or play normal
chess (click a piece, click a square). Full rules: castling, en passant,
promotion, check/checkmate. Sound toggle, strength selector, new game.

100% offline — nothing leaves your computer. Have fun, wizard. ✨
