WIZARD'S CHESS  —  Harry Potter style animated chess
=====================================================

TWO WAYS TO PLAY (pick either):

 1) EASIEST — just double-click  WizardsChess.html
    It opens in your web browser and plays instantly. No install, no internet.

 2) LOCALHOST — double-click  "Play on localhost (Windows).bat"
    (or run:  python serve.py)
    Then open  http://localhost:8800  in your browser.

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
- You command the WHITE stones (bottom). Click a piece; its legal moves glow
  green (red ring = a capture). Click a square to move.
- Captured pieces rear up and SHATTER, just like the first film.
- The stone army (Black) is a real chess engine.

FEATURES
--------
- Full legal chess: castling, en passant, pawn promotion (you pick the piece),
  check / checkmate / stalemate.
- Strong AI with quiescence search + iterative deepening (it won't hang pieces).
- Strength selector: Apprentice / Hard / Master / Grandmaster.
- Play as White or Black (board flips).
- Move list (Spellbook), fallen-piece trays, sound effects (toggle with the
  speaker button), undo, new game.
- Tilted 3D board, stone pieces that glide to their squares.

Everything runs 100% on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.
Enjoy — and mind the queen. ♛
